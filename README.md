# Introduction to Custom GPTs – Use Case Definition

## Course
Prompt Engineering

## Submitted By
**Shaik Mohammad Shaheed**

## GPT Name
**Career Mentor GPT**

## Overview

This repository contains my assignment submission for the **Introduction to Custom GPTs** lesson.

## Problem Statement

Many college students and fresh graduates struggle with resume writing, interview preparation, LinkedIn optimization, and career planning. They often rely on multiple websites and receive inconsistent guidance.

## Proposed Solution

The **Career Mentor GPT** is a specialized Custom GPT designed to provide personalized career guidance. It helps users:

- Create ATS-friendly resumes
- Write customized cover letters
- Prepare for HR and technical interviews
- Improve LinkedIn profiles
- Receive personalized career advice
- Find learning resources for career growth

## Target Users

- College students
- Fresh graduates
- Internship seekers
- Entry-level job applicants

## Expected Outcomes

- Better resume quality
- Improved interview confidence
- Stronger LinkedIn profiles
- Personalized career guidance
- Increased employability

## Why a Custom GPT?

Unlike a general AI assistant, the Career Mentor GPT focuses specifically on career development. It provides consistent, career-focused responses and personalized guidance based on the user's goals and skills.

## Files

- `Custom_GPT_Use_Case_Definition.pdf`

## Learning Outcomes

This assignment demonstrates how a Custom GPT can solve a real-world problem by providing focused, personalized, and practical career assistance for students and job seekers.
